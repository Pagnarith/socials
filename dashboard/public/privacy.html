Privacy Policy

Homework Palette · Publisher Chakriya · Last updated: September 12, 2026

This policy covers the Homework Palette iPhone/iPad app and related Chakriya websites including homework.chakriya.net and social.chakriya.net (Social Ops).

Summary

We do not sell data, show ads, or use third-party analytics in the Homework Palette app. The app does not create child accounts on our servers. Optional names, worksheets, scores, and recordings stay on your device unless you choose to share them through Apple services.

Social Ops & TikTok Login Kit

Our Social Ops dashboard may use TikTok Login Kit so an authorized admin can connect the official @homeworkpalette TikTok account. When connected, we receive TikTok profile fields needed for ops metrics (such as open id, display name, username, avatar, bio, and public stats like follower count, likes count, following count, and video count).

We use this only to display account health on our internal ops dashboard. We do not sell TikTok data, build ad audiences from it, or access private messages.

App data on device

  • Optional child name and selected grade
  • Custom worksheets, voice notes, and teaching videos you create
  • Quiz scores and preferences (including Palette Pro progress)

Apple services

Subscriptions are processed by Apple (StoreKit). Optional worksheet sharing may use Apple iCloud (CloudKit).
