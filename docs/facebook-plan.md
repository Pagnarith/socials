# Facebook Plan — Homework Palette

## Goals

- Reach parents & caregivers in Cambodia and diaspora  
- Share demos, tips, and App Store CTAs  
- Grow toward in-stream monetization thresholds  

## Post types

- Native video demos (with captions)  
- Carousel: free vs Palette Pro  
- Poster / QR share (https://homework.chakriya.net/poster.html)  
- Link posts to homework.chakriya.net  

## Cadence

- 4–6 posts / week  
- Cross-post YouTube demos same day

- About / website: see `docs/platform-bios.md`  
- Website field: https://homework.chakriya.net/
