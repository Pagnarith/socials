# Content Strategy — Homework Palette

**Brand:** Homework Palette · **Publisher:** Chakriya  
**CTA:** App Store + https://homework.chakriya.net/

## Positioning

Ready-made homework for grades 1–6. Free library, builder, Khmer & English read-aloud, and lesson recorder. Palette Pro unlocks in-app quizzes.

## Pillars

| Pillar | Goal | Formats |
|--------|------|---------|
| App demos | Show product value | YouTube 3–8 min, Reels cuts |
| Parent tips | Trust & routine | Short tips, carousels |
| Bilingual moments | Khmer + EN families | Side-by-side clips |
| Lesson recorder | Feature differentiation | Screen + face PiP demos |
| Download CTA | Conversions | Poster QR, App Store links |

1. **Discover** — short tips & demos on TikTok/Reels/FB

4. **Upgrade** — Palette Pro when quizzes matter
