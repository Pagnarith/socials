# Financial Plan — Homework Palette

## Revenue Streams

1. **Social recognition** — YouTube ads, TikTok Creativity, Facebook in-stream, sponsorships  
2. **App Store** — Homework Palette free downloads + Palette Pro subscriptions  
3. **Community support** — Buy Me a Coffee, GitHub Sponsors, Patreon, Super Chat  

## Phases

| Phase | Window | Focus | Target |
|-------|--------|-------|--------|
| 1 Foundation | M1–3 | Platforms live, first demos, App Store CTA habit | $0 |
| 2 Early growth | M3–6 | Short-form reach, first downloads | $25–50/mo |
| 3 Monetization | M6–12 | YPP / FB ads / early Pro | $145–480/mo |
| 4 Scale | M12–18 | Brand deals + stronger Pro | $530–1,950/mo |
| 5 Authority | M18–24 | Full stack monetization | $1,700–5,000/mo |

## Unit Economics (directional)

- Free library drives acquisition and trust  
- Palette Pro is the primary product revenue  
- Social content exists to grow downloads and explain Pro
